How Goalkeepr keeps you on track

Hover a card to see how each piece fits together.

01

Organization

Every goal gets a day, a category, and a color. Filter your calendar by work, school, training, or anything else you track, so a glance at the month tells you where your time is actually going.

02

Thoughtful task-execution

Not every goal deserves the same weight. Star-rate each one from a quick errand to a big push, so the important work stays visible instead of getting buried in a flat to-do list.

03

Momentum

Your month fills in as you follow through, so progress is something you can actually see. Watching the streak build is its own reason to come back tomorrow.
